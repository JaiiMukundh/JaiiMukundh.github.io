01 AIEI 2027 · FORMCEPT

GitHub ↗ Paper ↗

Structured Event Extraction with Small Language Models

Supply-chain disruption extraction · two-stage pipeline

A resource-efficient pipeline that turns unstructured supply-chain news into schema-valid disruption events. DistilBERT triage filters candidate chunks, a LoRA-adapted Qwen2.5-1.5B model extracts events, and Outlines FSM decoding guarantees structurally valid JSON.

  • 18.46M LoRA adapters (1.2% of the 1.54B base), trained on 115 documents from a 175-document annotated extraction set.
  • Schema-valid JSON 23.33% → 100%; overall extraction F1 46.81% → 69.24%; Top-Level F1 58.40% → 83.97%.
  • DistilBERT triage runs in ~15 ms/chunk vs ~14.32 s for Qwen, routing ~80% of enterprise documents away and cutting Qwen workload ~5×.

04 Publication

A paper, and a working system.

Accepted · AIEI 2027

Building Resource-Efficient Structured Event Extraction Systems with Small Language Models

Jaii Mukundh A, Anuj Kumar, Suresh Srinivasan — FORMCEPT Technologies & Solutions

2027 IEEE International Conference on AI Engineering and Innovations (AIEI 2027) · Bangalore · 21–23 Jan 2027

A two-stage pipeline combining high-recall DistilBERT triage, LoRA adaptation of Qwen2.5-1.5B, FSM-constrained decoding, and selective hallucination validation for enterprise event extraction — with a 17× inference-cost reduction over hosting a 70B model.
